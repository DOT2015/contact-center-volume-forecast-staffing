# Data Dictionary

All data is synthetic, generated by `src/generate_data.py` (seed 42). Raw CSVs are written to `data/raw/` (gitignored; `make generate` recreates them). Up to 1,000 rows per table are committed in `data/sample/`.

## Time conventions

| Column | Clock |
|---|---|
| `fact_call_leg.arrival_ts`, `answered_ts`, `ended_ts` | **UTC from 2024-04-01; America/Chicago wall time before that** (pre-migration) |
| `fact_outage.start_ts`, `end_ts` | UTC |
| `fact_staffing_interval.interval_start` | America/Chicago wall time, 48 half-hours per day, including DST artifacts |
| `dim_date.date`, `dim_business_event.event_date` | calendar dates (Central) |

The center is open 07:00-21:00 Central every day except New Year's Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas.

## Raw tables

### fact_call_leg (≈480,000 rows, 2024-01-01 to 2025-12-31)
| Column | Notes |
|---|---|
| leg_id | PK |
| contact_id | One customer call. About 4% of contacts have a second leg after a transfer |
| customer_id | FK to dim_customer |
| queue_code | **Renamed at the 2025-05-01 IVR change.** Before: BILL, TECH, MOVE, CANC, OUTG. After: Billing, Tech_Support, Moves_NewService, Cancellations, Outage, plus a 'TS' alias for 15% of tech legs |
| arrival_ts / answered_ts / ended_ts | answered_ts is NULL for abandoned legs |
| handle_time_sec | talk + wrap seconds. **0.6% of abandoned legs have a value > 0** (should be 0) |
| abandoned_flag, transfer_flag | 0/1 |
| reason_code | per-queue reason codes |

**Missing data:** 2024-08-12 and 2024-08-13 have no legs (ETL failure).

### dim_customer (120,000)
customer_id, plan_tier (basic_100, plus_300, gig_1000), region (North, South, East, West), signup_date (13% signed up during 2024-2025), bill_cycle (1 or 15).

### dim_queue (5)
queue_id, queue_name (billing, tech_support, moves_new_service, cancellations, outage), sl_target 0.80, sl_threshold_sec 60.

### fact_outage (90)
outage_id, region, start_ts, end_ts (UTC), customers_affected (lognormal, median about 3,000, range 150-40,000). Starts fall between 07:00 and 17:00 local.

### dim_business_event (30)
event_date, event_type (holiday, promo, price_notice), queues_affected, is_closed. The list includes the scheduled 2026-02-02 price notice.

### fact_staffing_interval (175,440)
interval_start (local), queue_id, agents_scheduled, agents_available. The rows are 731 days x 48 half-hours x 5 queues. **DST:** on 2024-11-03 and 2025-11-02 the 01:00 and 01:30 rows appear twice; on 2024-03-10 and 2025-03-09 the 02:00 and 02:30 rows do not exist. Closed hours have 0 agents.

### dim_date (821)
date (2024-01-01 to 2026-03-31), dow, is_holiday, is_dst_transition.

## Planted signals (generator parameters)

| Signal | Value |
|---|---|
| Day-of-week multipliers (normalized to mean 1) | Mon 1.35, Tue 1.15, Wed 1.05, Thu 1.00, Fri 0.95, Sat 0.55, Sun 0.45 before normalization |
| Intraday | 10:00-11:30 at 1.6x and 13:00-14:30 at 1.3x the interval mean; a smooth curve elsewhere, rescaled to mean 1 |
| Trend | +0.6% per month (compound) |
| Billing cycle | billing queue +22% on days 1-3 after the 1st and 15th (the 2nd-4th and 16th-18th) |
| Outages | 0.004 x customers_affected tech-support calls over 4 hours (truncated exponential, 1-hour time constant) |
| Price notice 2025-09-15 | billing and cancellations x (1 + 0.40 x 0.5^(k/3)) for days k = 0..9; AHT +8% for 14 days |
| Holidays (open) | x0.70 volume; promotions: moves/new service +15% for 7 days |
| Arrivals | Poisson given the intensity, per day x queue x half-hour |
| AHT | lognormal, sigma 0.55, medians: billing 4.2, tech 6.5, moves 7.5, cancellations 5.5, outage 3.0 minutes |

## Generator assumptions for queueing (documented, simplified)

- **Staffing history:** a simulated planner staffs each interval to Erlang C 80/60. It plans on a forecast that freezes the trend at the start of each year (+2%) and ignores outages and the price notice. Planning noise is 4%, and unplanned absence removes 5% of scheduled agents.
- **Waits:** each half-hour cell is treated as M/M/c with the available agents. Waits are drawn from the Erlang C waiting-time distribution. Overloaded cells use a 0.5-agent minimum surplus. Patience is exponential with a mean of 900 s, and a call abandons if its wait exceeds its patience.
